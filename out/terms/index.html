3. What 99 Crib does and does not do

99 Crib is a platform that connects Students with Landlords, verifies Listings in person, and protects payments through escrow. We are not the landlord, the agent or the owner of any property, and we are not a party to the tenancy between a Student and a Landlord.

We inspect each Listing before it goes live, but an inspection is a check of what is shown on the day. It is not a survey, a structural report or a check of legal title. Students should still read the tenancy terms and inspect the space themselves before paying.

4. Listings and verification

  • Listing is free. We do not charge to list a space.
  • Rent range: rent must be between ₦300,000 and ₦5,000,000 per year. We do not accept a Listing below ₦300,000 per year.
  • Full address: you must give the street address of the property, not just the university or area.
  • Accurate content: the room type, price, photos, videos and description must be true and current. You must have the right to let the property.
  • Verification: every Listing must pass an in-person verification by our team before it goes live. You agree to give our team access at a time arranged with you.
  • Failed verification: if a Listing fails verification, it is deleted from the platform. Any boost fee paid for that Listing is not refunded.
  • Room types: Self-Contain, Single Room, Shared Room, Hostel, Mini Flat and 2 Bed.

5. Premium boost

A Landlord may pay to boost a Listing so that it appears in the Premium carousel on the home screen for students at the relevant university. A boost costs ₦5,000 for one week or ₦15,000 for one month, per Listing. Boosts are bought for each Listing separately. A boost improves visibility, but we do not promise a number of views, enquiries or bookings. Boost fees are not refundable once the boost has started, and are not refunded if the Listing fails verification.

6. Fees and the Total Package

Students. You see the Rent Fee and the Total Package. The Total Package is the Rent Fee plus the caution fee, the agreement fee and a 5% service fee. The Total Package is the amount you pay through the app, and it is shown before you confirm.

Landlords. We charge a commission of 10% of the rent on each rent paid through 99 Crib. The commission is taken from the amount released to you.

All amounts are in Nigerian naira. If we change our fees, the change applies to new bookings and boosts, and does not change a booking that is already paid.

7. Payments and escrow

  • Pay in the app only. All rent payments must be made through 99 Crib. Never pay a Landlord, agent or anyone else outside the app, by transfer, cash or any other way. If you do, we cannot protect you, and we may suspend the accounts involved.
  • Payment partner. Payments, wallet savings and escrow funds are processed through our licensed payment partner, Rubies Payment.
  • How escrow works. When a Student pays, the money is held and is not sent to the Landlord. The funds are released to the Landlord only after the Student and 99 Crib have both confirmed move-in. 99 Crib then authorises the release.
  • Timing. Release happens after both confirmations are received. Processing times depend on the payment partner and banks.
  • Move-in confirmation. Students should confirm move-in only when the space matches the Listing. Confirming move-in affects refunds, see section 8.

8. Cancellations and refunds

  • Refunds are available only before move-in. Once move-in is confirmed, payments are not refundable through the app.
  • To ask for a refund, contact support with your booking reference. We will tell you what will be returned and how long it takes. The refund terms shown in the app at the time of booking apply.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method or to your wallet, and may take time because of the payment partner and banks.
  • If something is seriously wrong on move-in day, such as the property not matching the Listing, tell us before you confirm move-in so we can investigate.

9. Wallet and "pay small small"

  • The wallet lets a Student save towards rent in small amounts, so you can pay when you are ready. It is a savings feature. It is not a loan or credit, it pays no interest, and you never owe 99 Crib money for using it.
  • Saving in your wallet does not reserve a room. A space is secured only when a booking payment is completed.
  • Wallet funds are held with our licensed payment partner, Rubies Payment. The wallet is not a bank account.
  • If you withdraw wallet savings, you receive 95% of the amount withdrawn.
  • You can use your wallet balance towards the Total Package when you book.

10. Splitting a booking

For Self-Contain, Single Room, Mini Flat and 2 Bed Listings, two Students can split one booking equally. The first Student pays their half, and the space is held for 48 hours while the second Student pays theirs. If the second Student does not pay in time, the first Student's payment is returned in full. A split booking is one booking with one escrow, and both Students are covered by these Terms.

11. Roommate matching and accommodation requests

  • Roommate matching is optional. You see other Students at your own university and can message them. You choose what appears on your profile.
  • Details such as religion or ethnicity are optional and are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. You must not use them to harass or discriminate against anyone.
  • We do not vet roommates and we do not guarantee any match. Decide whom to live with carefully, and meet in safe, public places first.
  • An accommodation request tells us your budget (₦300,000 to ₦5,000,000 per year), preferred area and room type so we can suggest matching Listings. It does not guarantee a Listing.

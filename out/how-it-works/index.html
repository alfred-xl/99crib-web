How 99 Crib works

Simple, safe, and transparent from start to finish

Whether you're a student looking for a verified home near campus or a landlord wanting reliable tenants, 99 Crib makes the process straightforward and secure.

  1. 01

    Download the app and sign up with your university

    See listings near your campus that our team has inspected in person.

  2. 02

    Watch property reels

    Browse photo galleries and video reels shot during our verification visit. See the living area, bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, and surroundings so you know what you're getting before you pay.

  3. 03

    Pay securely, with funds held in escrow

    When you're ready, pay through the app. Your money is held in escrow and doesn't go to the landlord until move-in is confirmed.

  4. 04

    Move in with confidence

    Check that the space matches the listing. Funds are released to the landlord once move-in is confirmed, not before.

Changed your mind?

Contact support before move-in is confirmed and we'll guide you through your cancellation.
